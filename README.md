# Solar System!
All the planets [plus Pluto] in our system revolving around the center sun, with a controllable rocket ship for you to steer, equipped with a flashlight!

## Controls:
Left click to rotate the camera  
Right click to pan the camera  
Scroll to zoom in/out  
Arrow keys to move your ship!

## Features:
Orbiting & revolving planets, each with their own trajectory & speed [Pluto being the most interesting]  
Controllable rocket ship, equipped with a front light for viewing
Spinning asteroid belt
Staaaaars
A moon for the earth
